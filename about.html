About Lingchen

Building the future of digital innovation with passion, expertise, and unwavering commitment to excellence

Our Company Story

Zhengzhou Lingchen Network Technology Co., Ltd. was founded with a clear vision: to bridge the gap between cutting-edge technology and practical business solutions. Located in the heart of Zhengzhou's Guancheng Hui District, we have grown from a small startup into a comprehensive technology provider serving clients across 50+ countries worldwide.

Our journey began with a simple belief - that technology should empower businesses, not complicate them. This belief has guided every decision we make, every product we develop, and every service we provide. Today, we are proud to be at the forefront of network technology innovation, mobile application development, and digital transformation.

With over 100 mobile applications published on Google Play and Apple App Store, and a portfolio of satisfied enterprise clients, Lingchen Network Technology stands as a testament to what can be achieved when innovation meets dedication.
