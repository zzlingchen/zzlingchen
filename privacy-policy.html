5. Types of Advertisements in Our Apps

Our mobile applications may display the following types of advertisements:

5.1 Splash Ads (App Open Ads)

Full-screen advertisements displayed when users open or close the application. These ads appear during app launch or when returning to the app after backgrounding.

5.2 Rewarded Video Ads

Video advertisements that users can voluntarily watch to receive in-app rewards such as virtual currency, bonus features, or ad-free experiences. Users must actively choose to watch these ads.

5.3 Interstitial Ads

Full-screen advertisements that appear at natural transition points within the app, such as between levels in games or between content pages.

5.4 Banner Ads

Rectangular advertisements displayed at the top or bottom of the app screen. These ads remain visible while users navigate through the app.

5.5 Native Ads

Advertisements designed to match the visual style and format of the app's content, appearing organically within the app's interface.

5.6 Playable Ads

Interactive advertisements that allow users to try a brief demo of the advertised app or game before downloading.

5.7 Offerwall Ads

Advertisements presenting users with offers, surveys, or tasks they can complete in exchange for in-app rewards.

Note: We are committed to providing a balanced user experience and ensure that advertisements are clearly identifiable and do not interfere with the core functionality of our applications.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide. Our apps are not directed to children under 13 years of age (16 in certain jurisdictions like the EU).

For Apps Potentially Accessed by Children:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent
  • We use verified parental consent mechanisms where required
  • Parents can review, request deletion of, or refuse further collection of their child's information
  • We limit data collection to what's necessary for participation
  • Our kid-safe advertising partners (like SuperAwesome) are used where applicable

If you believe we have collected information from a child without parental consent, please contact us immediately at support@zzlingchen.com.

10. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to Access: Request copies of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request limitation of data processing
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time

Our legal basis for processing includes:

  • Performance of a contract
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Legitimate business interests
  • Your explicit consent

We have appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO). For GDPR-related inquiries, contact us at support@zzlingchen.com.

11. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of data collection and sharing practices
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of the sale of personal information
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Equal service regardless of exercising rights

Categories of personal information we collect include:

  • Identifiers (name, email, device ID)
  • Commercial information (purchases, transactions)
  • Internet activity information (browsing, app usage)
  • Geolocation data

To exercise your CCPA rights, contact us at support@zzlingchen.com or call our privacy hotline.
